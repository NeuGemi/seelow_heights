[b]The Battle of the Seelow Heights[/b] is a mod aimed at creating a historically immersive recreation of one of the most chaotic months of [i]World War II.[/i] Set either various months before, or a mere four days before the [b]Battle of Berlin[/b], or during the Battle of Berlin, play as either the German Reich or Japanese Empire and challenge yourself to see how long you can hold off the Allied onslaught.

[img]https://imgur.com/O70a70Z.gif[/img]
- January 12, 1945 Scenario
- March 6, 1945 Scenario
- April 12, 1945 Scenario
- April 27, 1945 Scenario
- National Focus Tree for Germany
- National Focus Tree for Japan
- Immersive Events
- Challenging Timed Missions
- Historically accurate Order of Battles
- Historical Division Templates
- New and Accurate Generals Roster for Germany
- And more

[img]https://imgur.com/iDDRGPW.gif[/img]
[b]World War II is coming to an end.[/b] The Wehrmacht is defeated, retreating on all fronts as the Allied and Soviet onslaught continues. The Allies rush towards the Elbe River, while the Red Army shells Berlin. Losing on every front, Goebbels calls for a total war and a final victory, to no avail.

In the Pacific, the disastrous Operation Ten-Go has crippled Japan's once-mighty fleet. US naval landings on Iwo Jima, Okinawa, and the Philippines have further cornered them. The success of Operation Ichi-Go offers a glimmer of hope, raising questions about whether Japan can end the war in China before the US reaches Tokyo.

[b]Is this mod compatible with an existing or old save?[/b] 
- It is not.
[b]Is this mod compatible with ‘X’ Mod?[/b]
- Generally, due to the amount of files changed, it is not compatible with any form of larger mod. Smaller, or Ironman-compatible mods usually work but should be proceeded with caution in mind.

For the full experience, play with all Expansion DLCs enabled.
